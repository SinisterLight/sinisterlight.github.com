Stevens

We’re developing a full-duplex, speech-to-speech model, customised to run locally on household hardware, able to listen while it speaks, respond naturally and adapt. As this intelligence becomes part of our most sensitive lives, our families, health, wealth, children and elders, personal identity and privacy will be of utmost importance.
